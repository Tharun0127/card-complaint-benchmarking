# Memo: what card members complain about, and what Amex should fix first

**To:** US Consumer Services, membership experience
**Data:** 142,540 CFPB credit card complaints about seven issuers, 2023-01-01 to 2026-08-31

## Answer

Amex complaints are less about fraud and payments than its peers' and more about membership value: whether the
customer got the offer, the points and the fee treatment they expected. On those same topics Amex gives money
back less often than peers do. The 2025 Platinum refresh has not raised the share of fee and benefit complaints
so far. The Chase Sapphire Reserve refresh did raise Chase's.

## What the data shows

**1. Amex against six peers.** Promotional terms not received is 7.0% of Amex complaints
and 2.8% of peer complaints (2.5x). Rewards problems are
6.3% against 3.2% (2.0x).
Amex is under-indexed on unauthorized charges and payment processing. Amex closes 14.9%
of complaints with monetary relief, which ranks 4 of 7. 

**2. After the refreshes.** In the 12 months after the Sapphire Reserve refresh, fee, rewards and promotional
terms complaints went from 16.6% to 20.0% of Chase complaints
while five comparison issuers went from 13.8% to 13.2%
(difference in differences +4.0 points, 95% interval 2.6 to
5.5, outside the placebo range). Rewards drove it (+2.8 points), and
narratives naming Sapphire Reserve went from 2.7% to 5.7%.
At Amex the same share went from 23.9% to 22.6% after the Platinum
refresh, and annual fee mentions stayed near 10% of narratives. This is suggestive,
not causal, and existing Platinum members only started renewing at USD 895 in January 2026.

## Fix first, in this order

1. **Make welcome offer eligibility clear before the application is submitted.** Promotional terms complaints are 7.0% of Amex complaints against 2.8% at peers. Monetary relief 9.0% against 20.5%.
2. **Post rewards reliably and warn before points are lost.** Rewards complaints are 6.3% against 3.2% at peers. Monetary relief 11.5% against 20.6%.
3. **Set a clear annual fee refund and renewal notice policy ahead of Platinum renewals.** 35% of Amex fee narratives are about the annual fee against 9% at peers. Monetary relief on fee complaints 24.4% against 38.7%.
4. **Make statement credits easy to track and fix posting-date edge cases.** Statement credit mentions went from 3.0% to 4.5% of Amex narratives after the refresh. An early signal.
5. **Explain credit limit reductions when they happen.** Credit limit complaints are 2.4% against 1.4% at peers. The smallest of the five.

## How much to trust this

- Complaints are self-selected. They show what goes wrong badly enough to escalate, not how often it goes wrong.
- Shares are compared instead of rates, because issuers' purchase volumes are not defined the same way.
- The CFPB does not record the card product, so product-level claims rest on keyword mentions in narratives.
- The LLM theme classification has not been run yet (no API key), so nothing in this memo depends on it. The findings above use CFPB fields and keyword counts.

and rewards posting also lead internal contacts, the first two fixes are sized and ready to prioritize.
