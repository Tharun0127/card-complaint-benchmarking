3. Refresh event study

Did complaints about fees, credits and benefits change after each premium card refresh? The refreshed issuer is compared with five issuers that had no refresh. This is suggestive, not causal: complaints are self-selected, the CFPB does not record the card product, the fee increases reached existing cardholders months after launch, and other things changed in the same months.
